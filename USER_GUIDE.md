# HireFlow User Guide

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Go to HireFlow website
2. Click "Get Started" or "Sign Up"
3. Enter your email and password
4. Verify your email if required
5. Choose your role:
   - **Job Seeker** — Track your job applications
   - **Employer** — Manage hiring pipeline

---

## Job Seeker Features

### Dashboard
Your dashboard shows:
- **Stats Cards** — Total applications, interviews, offers, response rate
- **Kanban Board** — Visual pipeline of all your applications
- **Search & Filters** — Find specific applications quickly

### Kanban Board

The Kanban board has 6 columns:
- **Unapplied** — Jobs you've discovered
- **Wishlist** — Jobs you want to apply to
- **Applied** — Applications submitted
- **Interview** — Interviews scheduled
- **Offer** — Offers received
- **Rejected** — Applications declined

**To move a card:**
1. Drag and drop the card to another column
2. The status updates automatically

**Card actions** (click the menu icon):
- Edit application details
- Add contact information
- Track documents
- Set reminders
- View activity history
- Match resume to job

### Job Discovery

1. Click "Job Discovery" in the navigation
2. Enter keywords and location
3. Browse results from multiple job boards
4. Click "Save Job" to add to your saved jobs
5. Click "Add to Kanban" to import directly

### AI Career Copilot

1. Click "AI Copilot" in the navigation
2. Ask questions about your job search
3. Get AI-powered advice and insights

### Resume Studio

1. Click "Resume Studio" in the navigation
2. Create or upload your resume
3. Use AI to improve your resume
4. Tailor resumes for specific jobs

### Interview Center

1. Click "Interview Center" in the navigation
2. Schedule interviews
3. Practice with AI interviewer
4. Get feedback on your answers

### Analytics

1. Click "Analytics" in the navigation
2. View your job search performance
3. Track application metrics
4. Get AI insights

## Employer Features

### Dashboard
Your employer dashboard shows:
- **Candidate Pipeline** — Visual pipeline of all candidates
- **Email Digest** — Scan for application emails
- **Team Management** — Invite team members

### Candidate Pipeline

The pipeline has 6 stages:
- **New** — New candidates
- **Reviewed** — Candidates reviewed
- **Interview** — Interviews scheduled
- **Offer** — Offers extended
- **Hired** — Hired candidates
- **Rejected** — Rejected candidates

**To move a candidate:**
1. Drag and drop to another stage
2. For Interview stage, scheduling assistant opens

### Team Collaboration

1. Create an organization
2. Invite team members
3. Assign roles (Admin, Recruiter, Hiring Manager, etc.)
4. Collaborate on candidates

### Email Integration

1. Connect your Gmail
2. Scan for application emails
3. Auto-create candidates from emails

### Analytics

1. View hiring metrics
2. Track recruiter performance
3. Get AI insights on hiring
