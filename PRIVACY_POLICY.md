# HireFlow Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 22, 2026

## Overview

HireFlow ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our platform.

---

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
- Email address
- Name
- Role (job seeker or employer)
- Profile picture (if provided via social login)

### Job Seeker Data
- Job applications and status
- Saved jobs and preferences
- Resume content
- Interview notes and schedules
- Contact information

### Employer Data
- Organization details
- Candidate information
- Hiring pipeline data
- Team member information
- Job postings

### Usage Data
- Pages visited
- Features used
- Search queries
- AI interactions
- Export activities

### Technical Data
- IP address (for security)
- Browser type
- Device information
- Login timestamps

---

## How We Use Your Data

### Primary Uses
- Provide and maintain the HireFlow service
- Process job applications and candidate management
- Enable AI-powered features (resume analysis, email classification)
- Process payments and subscriptions
- Send service-related communications
- Improve platform features and performance

### AI Features
- Your data is processed by Groq AI for features like:
  - Resume analysis and matching
  - Email classification
  - Interview preparation
  - Career recommendations
- AI processing is temporary; we do not store AI prompts long-term
- You control what data is shared with AI features

### Communication
- Service updates and announcements
- Account-related notifications
- Marketing communications (with consent)

## Data Sharing

### Third-Party Services
We share data with these services to provide functionality:

| Service | Purpose | Data Shared |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Clerk | Authentication | Email, name |
| Stripe | Payments | Email, subscription status |
| Groq | AI features | Resume text, job descriptions |
| Google (Gmail) | Email integration | Email metadata (with consent) |
| Google (Calendar) | Calendar integration | Calendar events (with consent) |

### We Do NOT Sell Your Data
We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

---

## Data Security

### Protection Measures
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Encryption at rest (database-level)
- Secure authentication (Clerk)
- Regular security audits
- Access controls and permissions
- Input validation and sanitization

### Data Storage
- Primary: PostgreSQL database (Neon)
- Backups: Automatic daily backups
- Retention: Data retained while account is active

## Your Rights

### Access
- View all your data in the dashboard
- Export your data as CSV

### Deletion
- Delete your account at any time
- All personal data will be removed
- Some data may be retained for legal compliance

### Correction
- Update your profile information
- Edit application and candidate data

### Portability
- Export your data as CSV
- Request full data export
