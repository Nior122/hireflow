# HireFlow Pricing Strategy

## Pricing Philosophy

HireFlow uses a **value-based pricing** model that aligns with customer success:
- Free tier for product-led growth
- Affordable paid plans for individuals
- Team-based pricing for organizations
- Enterprise pricing for large deployments

---

## Plan Structure

### Free Plan — $0/month
**Target:** New users, students, casual job seekers

**Features:**
- 5 job applications
- Basic kanban board
- 10 AI requests/day
- 2 CSV exports/month
- Basic search and filters
- Mobile responsive

**Limits:**
- No resume studio
- No interview center
- No email scanning
- No team features

**Purpose:** Product-led growth, show value before asking for payment

---

### Pro Plan — $19/month ($190/year)
**Target:** Active job seekers, career changers

**Features:**
- Unlimited applications
- Resume Studio (create, edit, optimize)

- Email scanning (Gmail integration)
- Job Discovery (multi-provider search)
- Analytics dashboard

- 100 AI requests/day

**Upgrade Triggers:**
- Reaching 5 application limit
- Wanting to use AI features
- Need for resume optimization
- Wanting email scanning

## Pricing Psychology

### Anchoring
- Show Enterprise plan first to anchor high
- Pro plan appears affordable by comparison

### Loss Aversion
- "You've used 4 of 5 free applications"
- "Your AI analysis is 80% complete"
- "Your competitors are using AI-powered tools"

### Social Proof
- "Join 10,000+ job seekers using HireFlow"
- "Trusted by companies like [logos]"

### Urgency
- "Save 17% with annual billing"
- "Limited time: Get 3 months free on annual plans"

1. **App limit reached** → Show upgrade prompt
