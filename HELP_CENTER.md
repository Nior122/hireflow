# HireFlow Help Center

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account
1. Go to hireflow.com
2. Click "Sign Up"
3. Enter your email and password
4. Choose your role (Job Seeker or Employer)
5. Start using HireFlow!

### First Steps for Job Seekers
1. Add your first application using the "+" button
2. Drag applications through the Kanban board
3. Try the AI Career Copilot for tips
4. Connect Gmail to auto-import applications

### First Steps for Employers
1. Create your organization in Team Dashboard
2. Invite team members
3. Create a job posting
4. Add or receive candidates

## Common Questions

### How do I add a job application?
1. Click the "+ Add Application" button
2. Enter company name, role, and other details
3. Click "Add Application"
4. The card appears in the "Unapplied" column

### How do I move applications?
Simply drag and drop the card from one column to another. The status updates automatically.

### How does AI email scanning work?
1. Click "Email Digest" in the employer dashboard
2. Click "Connect Gmail" and authorize access
3. Click "Scan Inbox" to find job-related emails
4. AI classifies and you can import matching emails

### How do I export my data?
1. Click the "Export" button in the toolbar
2. Choose CSV format
3. Your data downloads automatically

### Can I use HireFlow on mobile?
Yes! HireFlow is responsive and works on mobile browsers. A native mobile app is also in development.

## Troubleshooting

### "Gmail not connected"
1. Click "Connect Gmail" in the Email Digest panel
2. Complete the OAuth authorization
3. Return to HireFlow

### "AI service unavailable"
AI features require a valid Groq API key. Contact support if this persists.

### "Application not found"
Refresh the page. If the issue persists, check that you're logged into the correct account.

### Slow performance
1. Clear your browser cache
2. Check your internet connection
3. Try a different browser
