## Email Design Guidelines

### Style
- Clean, modern design
- Mobile-responsive
- Consistent branding
- Clear CTAs
- Minimal text

### Best Practices
- Subject lines: 30-50 characters
- Preheader text: 40-100 characters
- Send time: 9-11 AM recipient time
- Frequency: Max 3 emails/week
- Unsubscribe: Easy one-click
